# Product: Graveyard of Ideas (working name)

A personal app for abandoned projects, presented as a small first-person 3D night graveyard that serves a real function: keep ideas, understand why they stopped, and organize a realistic restart.

## Core loop
Create a small book -> bury it in a tomb -> find the tomb -> open the book -> reread and prepare a restart -> book dissolves into embers -> tomb becomes a black specter that flies away -> continue the project in the central grimoire.

## Principles
- Every action has a logic in the world: a book is created, placed in a tomb, taken out to be read, then transformed on resurrection. The scenery carries the function; it is never just decoration around forms.
- Dark, haunted and beautiful, but reading and writing stay simple and legible.
- The graveyard only contains resting projects. A resurrected project is neither deleted nor duplicated.
- The app never guilts the user and never punishes absence. No attacks, no inactivity penalties, no automatic loss.
- Animations accompany actions but never block them. Every cinematic can be skipped and lands on the correct final state.
- A light, non-3D path keeps every essential function available.

## Three statuses
Resting (tomb + book), Resurrected (grimoire only), Accomplished (grimoire only). Statuses are data; animations only represent them.

## Non-goals for the prototype
Multiplayer, accounts, cloud sync, AI features, infinite world, full physics, character hands, complex task boards, sound (added last).

## Tone of text
Short, calm, slightly poetic. English only (judges read English).
